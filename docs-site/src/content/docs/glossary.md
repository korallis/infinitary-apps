These are the words Infinitary uses with a meaning of its own, in alphabetical order.
Each one links to the page that explains it in full.

## A

### Activity

The panel at the bottom of the window's left side, with one line for each thing your first mate and your crew do.
A task's own lines are on the **Activity** tab of its card.
See [Panels](/panels/#the-apps-own-panels).

### Allowance

How much of your AI plan is left before it resets, such as for a session or a week.
Settings → **Intelligence** shows it as a meter on each account.
See [Subscriptions and allowance](/subscriptions-and-quota/#read-your-plans-allowance).

### Ask before merging

A project's switch that decides who merges its finished work.
When it is on, the work waits for your click on its merge card; when it is off, work that passes the checks may merge without asking you.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/#asking-before-merging).

### Automation

A prompt your first mate gets on a schedule, or each time something happens, such as a task closing.
See [Automation](/automation/).

## B

### Backlog

Where a task waits until you press **Start** on it.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/#where-a-task-stands).

### Batch

For a project whose changes go as **batched pull requests**, one pull request that combines your approved changes, one commit for each, and merges once its checks pass.
A change waiting for its batch shows "batch" in Tasks.
See [Projects](/projects/#how-changes-reach-your-project).

### Branch

A separate line of changes in a project kept with git.
Each crewmate makes its changes on a branch of its own, and merging brings them into your project.
See [Projects](/projects/#working-copies).

## C

### Card

How your first mate or a crewmate brings you one answer, one finding or one decision, with the evidence behind it.
Cards come up in the middle of the window, one at a time, and each is a [note](#note) or a [decision](#decision).
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/).

### Checks

The tests and other checks your project runs on a change before it merges.
**Merge when checks pass** waits for them, and a crewmate fixes them when they fail.
See [Your first task](/your-first-task/#5-merge-it).

### Command search

A search box that finds any command in Infinitary and shows its keys.
Press ⌘K on a Mac, or Ctrl+K on Linux and Windows.
See [Keyboard](/keyboard/#command-search).

### Computer that runs work

Another computer you added that does your projects' work, with no window open there and with its own subscriptions.
**Runs on**, in Settings → **Projects**, picks the computer for each project.
See [Your other computers](/your-other-computers/#a-computer-that-runs-work).

### Computer where your first mate lives

The one computer that keeps your first mate, with its tasks, cards and conversation.
Your other computers and your phone reach your first mate there, so it needs to stay awake with Infinitary open.
See [Your other computers](/your-other-computers/#the-computer-where-your-first-mate-lives).

### Controlling

What another computer you added does while Infinitary is open there: it shows your first mate from the computer where it lives.
The top of its window reads "Controlling your first mate on" and that computer's name.
See [Your other computers](/your-other-computers/#control-your-first-mate-from-another-computer).

### Copy of your first mate

A live copy of your first mate's record, kept by a computer that runs work, so that computer can [take over](#take-over).
See [Your other computers](/your-other-computers/#keep-a-copy-on-another-computer).

### Crew's browser

A browser a crewmate has for its own task, kept apart from your own browser and its sign-ins.
You can watch it and take control, and a purchase or a booking is always yours to make.
See [The crew's browser](/the-crews-browser/).

### Crewmate

An AI helper that does one task's work, in its own working copy of your project.
Your crew is all your crewmates together, and your first mate runs it.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/).

## D

### Decision

A card that asks you to choose.
It offers two or three choices, and the one its author recommends is the main button.
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/#notes-and-decisions).

### Diagnostics

The one switch that sends anything to us: anonymous usage events, with no text you wrote, no names and no paths.
It is in Settings → **General**, under **Sharing**.
See [What leaves your computer](/what-leaves-your-computer/#diagnostics-the-one-switch-that-sends-to-us).

## F

### First mate

The one you talk to in Infinitary, drawn in the middle of the window.
It answers you, turns work into tasks, starts a crewmate on each and brings you cards, and it never edits your files itself.
See [The first mate](/the-first-mate/).

### Focus

Narrowing the window to one project, from the project pill in the top bar.
Tasks, Activity and cards then show only that project, and new work you ask for goes there unless you name another.
See [Projects](/projects/#focus-on-one-project).

## I

### Integration

A connection to something outside your projects, such as GitHub, Gmail or an MCP server, signed in as you.
Your first mate, your crew, your automations and your panels all reach it the same way.
See [Integrations](/integrations/).

## K

### Knowledge

What your first mate and your crew keep for later work: standing directions, personal context and saved sources.
See [Knowledge](/knowledge/).

## M

### Merge

Bringing a finished change into your project.
You merge a change from its merge card, and a project that does not ask before merging may merge it without asking you.
See [Your first task](/your-first-task/#5-merge-it).

### Merge card

The card a crewmate brings when a change is ready.
It tells the change's story, with Before and After pictures and a drawing of its risk, and its buttons merge or discard the work.
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/#merge-cards).

### Message box

The box at the bottom of the window, where you type to your first mate or talk instead of typing.
Press ⌘L (Ctrl+L on Linux and Windows) to go to it from anywhere.
See [The first mate](/the-first-mate/#talking-to-your-first-mate).

## N

### Note

A card that tells you something and asks nothing.
Its one button is **Got it**, or **Done** when reading it closes the task it answers.
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/#notes-and-decisions).

## O

### Out of reach

Said of a computer or phone that Infinitary cannot reach right now, such as one asleep, one with Infinitary closed, or one on another network.
Work that needs it waits until it is back.
See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/#another-computer-is-out-of-reach).

## P

### Panel

A box on the side of the window, such as Activity, Tasks or one your first mate built for you.
See [Panels](/panels/).

### Personal context

What you tell your first mate about yourself, such as "I'm new to coding", kept so later work can use it.
See [Knowledge](/knowledge/#tell-your-first-mate-about-yourself).

### Plan

A card a crewmate brings before it builds something bigger, asking only the decisions that are yours to make.
**Approve and build** starts the work, and **Save and defer** keeps the plan for later.
See [What you can ask for](/what-you-can-ask-for/#a-plan-before-building).

### Private sites

Sites on your office, home or VPN network that you listed, so a task's browser may open them.
Your first mate never adds one itself: it asks you on a card.
See [The crew's browser](/the-crews-browser/#private-sites).

### Project

A folder of your own, kept with git, that your crew works on.
Every task belongs to a project.
See [Projects](/projects/).

### Pull request

A change offered for merging where your project lives, such as on GitHub or GitLab, where its checks run.
**Merge the pull request**, on its merge card, merges it.
See [Projects](/projects/#how-changes-reach-your-project).

### Put aside

Hiding a card without answering it.
It waits under **Waiting on you** in Tasks, and comes back when its author changes it.
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/#putting-a-card-aside).

## Q

### Queued

Where a task waits to start by itself once what it waits on is done, such as another task.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/#where-a-task-stands).

## R

### Record

What every task keeps: what you asked, what changed, the evidence and what you decided.
A task's record stays after the task closes.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/).

### Rigor

How thoroughly work is checked before it reaches you: **Quick**, **Default** or **Careful**.
Each project has its own, in Settings → **Projects**.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/#rigor).

### Runbook

Written guidance your first mate and your crew follow for a kind of work, such as planning a change or teaching a lesson.
It runs nothing on your computer.
See [Runbooks](/runbooks/).

## S

### Secret

A password or key your crew uses by its name, only once you allow that use.
Infinitary never shows the value again, and neither your first mate nor your crew gets it.
See [Secrets](/secrets/).

### Signed-in sites

The sites you signed in to in a task's browser, kept in that computer's browser profile.
For each one, you choose whether later tasks ask you first or use it without asking.
See [The crew's browser](/the-crews-browser/#signed-in-sites).

### Standing direction

How you want your work run from now on, in your own words, which your first mate keeps and follows.
It applies everywhere, or to one project.
See [Knowledge](/knowledge/#give-a-standing-direction).

### Subscription

An AI plan you already have, such as Codex or Claude, that your first mate and your crew work through.
Each one you sign in to is an account in Settings → **Intelligence**.
See [Subscriptions and allowance](/subscriptions-and-quota/).

## T

### Take over

Starting your first mate on a computer that keeps a copy of it, while the computer where it lives is out of reach.
Only your click starts it.
See [Your other computers](/your-other-computers/#take-over).

### Task

One piece of work your first mate tracks, with a key such as `WEB-12` that starts with its project's key.
Tasks, on the right of the window, lists them by where they stand.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/).

### Task board

A fuller view of your tasks: **Waiting on you** at the top, then the Backlog, Queued and Working columns.
Press **Open task board** in the Tasks header.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/#the-task-board).

### Thread

The conversation beside a card that your first reply opens.
The card's author answers you there.
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/#the-reply-field-and-the-thread).

## W

### Waiting on you

Where Tasks lists a task with a card that needs your decision.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/#where-a-task-stands).

### Working copy

A copy of your project that a crewmate works in, on its own branch, so your own files stay as they are until you merge.
See [Projects](/projects/#working-copies).
