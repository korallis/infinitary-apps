Infinitary is your own AI studio.
It is a desktop app for macOS, Linux and Windows.
You talk to one first mate, and a crew of AI crewmates does the work.

## How a task goes

1. You tell your first mate what you want in the message box.
2. Your first mate breaks the ask into tasks and gives each task to a crewmate.
3. Each crewmate works in its own working copy of your project, so your files stay where they are.
4. When a crewmate needs your decision, or has finished, it brings you a card.
5. You make the call on the card, and the crew carries on.

Decisions come to you as cards.
Your crew brings back just what you need to know, so the call is yours.

## Your own

Infinitary runs on your computer, through the AI subscriptions you already have.

- **Your computer.**
  The first mate, the crew and your projects live on your computer.
  Work happens in working copies of your own projects.
- **Your subscriptions.**
  Infinitary works through the subscriptions you already have.
  No credits and no metering: your provider's bill is your model bill.
- **Your record.**
  Every task leaves a record: what you asked, what changed, the evidence and what you decided.
