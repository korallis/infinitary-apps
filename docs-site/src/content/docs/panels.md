title: Panels
description: Panels sit on the left and right sides of the window and show your tasks, your crew's activity and anything else you ask your first mate to build.
---

A panel is a box on the side of the Infinitary window.
Panels sit on two rails, one on the left and one on the right, around your conversation with your first mate.
A rail with nothing to show takes no room.

Every panel has a header with its title.
Click the header's arrow to collapse a panel or expand it again.
A collapsed panel can still say, in a few words, what it holds, such as how many tasks wait for you.

## The app's own panels

Infinitary comes with these panels.

- **Activity**, at the bottom of the left rail.
  It shows what your first mate and your crew are doing, one line at a time.
- **Tasks**, at the bottom of the right rail.
  It lists your tasks and shows which ones are waiting for you.
  Its **Search tasks** button finds a task, and **Open task board** opens the whole board.
- **Knowledge**, under Tasks.
  It shows the standing directions your first mate keeps, and **Open Knowledge** opens the rest of what your crew has learned.
  It appears once there is some knowledge to show.
  See [Knowledge](/knowledge/).
- **Automation**, under Knowledge.
  It lists your automations and when each runs next.
  It appears once an automation is saved.
  See [Automation](/automation/).

## Arrange your panels

You can move any panel to another place on either rail.

- **On the rails.**
  Press a panel's header and drag it.
  A line shows where it will land.
  Let go there to move it, or let go anywhere else to put it back.
- **In Settings.**
  Open **Settings**, then **Panels**.
  The list shows each rail's top and bottom groups in order.
  Drag a row to move its panel, or use its switch to hide or show it.
  **Reset to defaults** puts every panel back where it started.
- **By asking.**
  Tell your first mate where you want a panel, such as "move Tasks to the left rail".

A panel you hide stays in the list in **Settings**, so you can show it again.
When a rail runs out of room, the panels nearest its middle fold first.
If even their headers do not fit, a row such as **2 more** lists the rest.
Pick one there to bring it back to the rail.

## Use the keyboard

- Press F6 to move to the next part of the window, a panel included.
  Shift+F6 moves back.
- Press ⌘, (Ctrl+, on Linux and Windows) to open **Settings**.

Some panels add commands of their own, such as **Find a task** and **Open Knowledge**.
You find them in command search and can give them a key in **Settings**, **Keyboard**.
See [Keyboard](/keyboard/).

## Panels your first mate builds for you

You can ask for a panel of your own.
Tell your first mate what it should show, such as "Make me a panel that shows the weather in Tokyo".
A crewmate builds it from the app's own parts, so it looks like the rest of Infinitary.

After your crew has finished three tasks, an empty **Create your own panel** box appears at the top of the left rail.
Click it to start the ask in the message box.
Click its **Hide** to put it away for good.

A panel you asked for lands at the top of the left rail unless you say where.
Its header says **yours**.
You move it like any other panel.
To change it, tell your first mate what you want different.
To take it away, ask your first mate to remove it.

## Turn your panels off

**Settings**, **General**, **Your panels** turns off every panel your first mate built for you at once.
The app's own panels stay.
Turn the switch back on to bring your panels back.

If Infinitary closes twice just after starting, it turns your panels off by itself, in case one of them was the cause.
The switch then says so.
Turn them back on when you are ready.

If one of your panels stops working, the panel says so, and the rest of the window carries on.
When a panel's work in the background stops responding, its **Retry** button starts it again.
