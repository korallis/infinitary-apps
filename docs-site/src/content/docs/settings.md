Open Settings with ⌘, (Ctrl+, on Linux and Windows), the **Settings** button at the top of the window, or **Open Settings** in command search.
The sections are listed down the left.
The up and down arrows move between them, and Escape or **Back** leaves Settings.

You can also ask your first mate to change a setting for you, such as "switch to the light theme" or "turn on ambient music".

## General

- **Start at login** opens Infinitary when you sign in to your computer.
- **Show in dock**, on a Mac, hides the dock icon when it is off, while the window stays.
- **Notify me when my first mate needs me** sends a quiet notification for decisions and reports while the window is in the background.
- **Dictation** lets you press or hold a key to talk instead of typing, turned into words on your computer.
  Off leaves the key and the microphone to other apps.
  See [The first mate](/the-first-mate/#talk-instead-of-typing).
- **Your panels** takes down every panel your first mate built for you, and the ones you added, until you turn it back on.
- **Purge task data** removes every task, with its working copy, its notes and its cards, and the knowledge your crew recorded.
  Press **Purge**, then **Confirm purge**.
- **Sharing** holds the **Diagnostics** switch, a line on what the update check tells us whatever that switch says, and **What would leave this computer**.
  See [What leaves your computer](/what-leaves-your-computer/#diagnostics-the-one-switch-that-sends-to-us).
- **Version** names the version you run, and opens each release note you were shown again.
  Under it, Infinitary says whether you are up to date, with a button such as **Check for updates**.
  See [Update and remove Infinitary](/update-and-remove/).

On a computer that controls your first mate from elsewhere, General also lists **Website sign-ins**: the sites you signed in to in a [task's browser](/the-crews-browser/#signed-in-sites) there.
On the computer where your first mate lives, they are under **Signed-in sites** in **Secrets**.

## Appearance

- **Size** makes text and everything around it larger, at 100, 110, 125 or 150%.
- **Default card size** chooses how cards open: **Usual**, **Wide**, **Full** or **Maximized**.
  You can still resize each card.
- **Keep cards maximized** keeps every card maximized after you maximize one, until you restore one.
- **Theme** is **Dark** or **Light**.
- **Window wash**, on a Mac, sets how much of the theme color covers the blurred background.
  0% is the default.
- **Crew model names** shows model names while your crewmates use only one orbit.

### Fonts

Infinitary has three fonts you can choose:

- **App font**: the words of the window, its panels, buttons and Settings.
- **Reading font**: what the crew writes on cards.
  It stays **Same as the app font** until you choose another.
- **Code font**: code, keys and task keys.

Each offers Infinitary's own font, **This computer's font**, or one of your **Installed fonts**.

### Language

**Language** sets the words Infinitary shows, and the language your first mate and crew write to you in.
English is the default.
The others are Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese as written in Brazil, and Arabic, each named in its own language.
Some parts of Infinitary still show English.

### Sound

**Ambient music** plays calm lofi made on your computer.
It follows the crew, and chimes when something waits for you.
It is off until you turn it on, and **Volume** sets how loud it plays.

A sound button sits just before **Settings** at the top of the window, whether the music is on or off.
Press it to turn the music on or off; the **Ambient music** switch turns with it.
Rest the pointer on it and it names the music that is playing; rest on the name, or press it, to pick another style from the list below it.
Command search has **Play or stop ambient music** and **Choose ambient music** too.

## Runbooks

A runbook is written guidance your first mate and crew follow for a kind of work.

- **Built in** lists the ones that come with Infinitary.
  **View** reads one, and its switch turns it on or off.
- **Yours** lists the runbooks you added or made with your first mate.
- **Make your own** has **Your runbooks folder**, where they live, with **Open folder**.
  To make one, ask your first mate in your own words.

[What you can ask for](/what-you-can-ask-for/) shows some of the work built-in runbooks guide, such as lessons and small projects for fun.
See [Runbooks](/runbooks/).
