---
title: The first mate
description: Who your first mate is, what it does and does not do, how to talk to it, and how to choose the model it answers with.
---

Your first mate is the one you talk to in Infinitary.
It is drawn in the middle of the window, and it runs your crew.

## What your first mate does

- It reads your message and decides what it needs: an answer, a question back to you, or work.
- It answers a question it can answer itself, on a card.
- It turns work into tasks and starts a crewmate on each one.
- It keeps track of every task, tells a crewmate what you said, and closes work when it is done.
- It brings you a card when something needs your decision.

## What it leaves to the crew

Your first mate does not edit your files, read your code, or run anything in your project.
Anything that needs your project's files, a build or a look at what went wrong goes to a crewmate.
Each crewmate works in its own working copy of your project, so your own files stay as they are.
See [Tasks and crewmates](/tasks-and-crewmates/).

## Talking to your first mate

Type in the message box at the bottom of the window and send it.
Your message and your first mate's short reply stay just above the box.
Click the reply to read all of it.

A few things help:

- **Go to the message box from anywhere.**
  Press ⌘L (Ctrl+L on Linux and Windows).
- **Bring back your last message.**
  Press ↑ in an empty message box.
- **Paste a lot of text.**
  A long paste becomes a file chip in the box, and goes with your message whole.

### Add pictures and files

Drag files onto the message box, or press **Add files**.
Pictures and text files go with your message, up to four of each.
For other kinds of file, such as a PDF, the box adds the file's location on your computer as text, so a crewmate can open it there.

### Talk instead of typing

Press or hold the dictation key to talk.
The hint in the message box names it: "type, or press/hold right ⌥ to talk".
On Linux and Windows the key is right Ctrl.
What you say is turned into text on your computer, and you can edit it before sending.
To change the key, or turn dictation off, use **Dictation** in [Settings → General](/settings/#general).

### Answer it on a card

When your first mate or a crewmate brings a card, answer on the card itself.
What you type under a card goes to whoever wrote it.
See [Cards and decisions](/cards-and-decisions/).

## Choosing its model

Your first mate answers through one of your subscriptions.
To choose which model, open Settings → Intelligence and use **First mate model**.
**Auto (from subscriptions)** lets Infinitary pick from what your subscriptions offer.
A change applies the next time your first mate is idle.

If you have two accounts with the same provider, the row reads **First mate model and account**, and you can pick the account too.
Your crew's work can prefer another account: use **Prefer for crew work** in that account's menu.
See [Subscriptions and allowance](/subscriptions-and-quota/).

If the subscription your first mate uses stops answering, for example because your plan's allowance is spent, your first mate shows one card that says why and what you can do.
Messages you send meanwhile are kept, and go through when it can answer again.

## Keep its computer on

Your first mate lives on one computer.
Your phone and your other computers reach it there.
Set it up on a computer that stays on as much as possible.

When that computer is asleep or Infinitary is closed there, your first mate cannot answer.
To open Infinitary when you sign in, turn on **Start at login** in Settings → General.

To reach your first mate from elsewhere, see [Your other computers](/your-other-computers/) and [iPhone and iPad](/iphone-and-ipad/).
