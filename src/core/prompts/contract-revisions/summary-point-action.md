# Summary point action

Settings → Intelligence gains the summary point, where the first mate and crew summarize a long conversation: Automatic (300,000 tokens, or below a model's first price step), 150,000, 200,000, 300,000, 500,000, or each model's own limit.
The captain chose that a number they pick wins over price steps, and that the row names the models that charge more past it.
The summary-point action line owns changing it when asked; its current value is the point and the price steps of the models on the person's accounts.
The action enum grows by one name; the line's words are read through app_state, not fixed context.
