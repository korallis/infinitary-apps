# An account row may say until when its provider refuses it

A quota account row may carry `refusedUntilMs`, set on the served row of a login of a pool of the person's own logins while its provider's refusal of that login stands.
A reader that names when such a login is back takes the later of that time and its used-up windows' own resets.
An older side ignores the field and reads the windows alone, as it did before.
