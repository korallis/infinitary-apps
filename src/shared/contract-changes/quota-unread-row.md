# An account row that no reading produced

A quota account row may carry `unread`, set when no reading of the allowance produced it: its windows are a provider's refusal laid over a reading that could not be taken, or an answer from the usage service or a proxy that carried windows and no share in any of them.
The first mate's pause card then says the allowance could not be read, never that it was checked.
A reader must not take an unread row's `measuredAt` as the time a share was read: it is the refusal's time or the answer's.
An older side ignores the field and reads the row as a reading, as it did before.
